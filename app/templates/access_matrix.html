Governance controls in force

Row-level scoping. Agency focal points and requesters are bound to a single counterpart and cannot read or act on another's bookings or invoices.
Segregation of duties. The role that approves bookings cannot grant waivers; waivers sit with finance. Neither can create user accounts.
Immutable charge snapshot. Charges are frozen on submission, so later rate-card edits cannot rewrite issued invoices.
Account protection. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256; accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts and admin-issued passwords must be changed.
Mandatory justification. Waivers require written justification recorded against the approving officer.
Full audit trail. Sign-ins, denials, approvals, waivers, rate changes and invoice transitions are all written to an append-only log.
